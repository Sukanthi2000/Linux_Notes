# 🧭 Linux vs Other Operating Systems
---------------------------------------

Operating systems (OS) act as a bridge between the user and the computer hardware.  
While there are many OS options, **Linux stands out** for its flexibility, security, and open-source foundation.

---
## 🏆 Why Linux Overshadows Other OS

| **Feature** | **Linux** | **Windows** | **macOS** |
|--------------|-----------|--------------|------------|
| **Cost** | Free & Open Source | Paid | Paid |
| **Customization** | Highly flexible | Limited | Very limited |
| **Security** | Very strong (user permission-based) | Vulnerable to malware | Secure but closed |
| **Performance** | Light & efficient | Heavy resource usage | Optimized for Apple devices |
| **Developer Support** | Massive community | Proprietary | Proprietary |

---

## 🌍 Real-world Dominance
--------------------------------
- **Servers:** 90%+ of world servers run Linux  
- **Cloud & DevOps:** All major cloud providers (AWS, GCP, Azure) use Linux-based systems  
- **Mobile:** Android is built on the Linux kernel  
- **Embedded systems:** From routers to smart TVs — Linux powers them all
